# HostelHub

## Problem

Students often buy and sell books, electronics, cycles, calculators, and hostel items through WhatsApp groups or Instagram stories.

Problems:
- Difficult to search 
- Fake buyers/sellers
- No product categories
- Listings disappear in chats
- No trusted platform

## Solution

HostelHub is a campus-only marketplace where verified students can buy and sell products safely.

## Target Users

- College students
- Hostel students
- Day scholars

## Vision

Become the one-stop platform for buying, selling, renting, and exchanging items within college campuses.
